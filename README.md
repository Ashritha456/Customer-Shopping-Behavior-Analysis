# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of customer shopping behavior using
Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.

The analysis is based on 3,900 customer purchase records across multiple product
categories. The objective is to understand customer purchasing patterns, identify
important customer segments, analyze product and subscription behavior, and turn
transactional data into actionable business insights.

The project demonstrates a complete analytics workflow:

**Raw Data → Python Data Cleaning → PostgreSQL → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand customer shopping behavior in order to:

- Improve sales
- Increase customer engagement
- Understand customer loyalty
- Identify high-performing products
- Evaluate discount effectiveness
- Understand subscription behavior
- Identify important customer segments
- Improve marketing and product strategies

The primary business question is:

> **How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends,
> improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 customer purchase records** and **18 columns**.

### Key Features

**Customer Information**
- Customer ID
- Age
- Gender
- Location
- Subscription Status

**Purchase Information**
- Item Purchased
- Category
- Purchase Amount
- Season
- Size
- Color

**Customer Behavior**
- Previous Purchases
- Purchase Frequency
- Review Rating
- Discount Applied
- Promo Code Used
- Shipping Type

The original dataset contained **37 missing values in Review Rating**.

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Exploratory Analysis — Python

The first stage of the project was completed in Python using Pandas.

### Data Cleaning

- Loaded and inspected the raw customer dataset
- Used `df.info()` and `df.describe()` to understand data structure
- Identified missing values
- Filled missing Review Rating values using the median rating for each product category
- Standardized column names using `snake_case`
- Checked data consistency and redundant variables
- Removed the redundant `promo_code_used` column

### Feature Engineering

Created additional analytical features:

- `age_group`
- `purchase_frequency_days`

After preprocessing, the cleaned DataFrame was loaded into **PostgreSQL** for SQL analysis.

## 🗄️ SQL Business Analysis

SQL queries were developed in PostgreSQL to answer key business questions.

### 1. Revenue by Gender

Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.

### 2. High-Spending Discount Customers

Identified customers who used discounts but still spent more than the overall
average purchase amount.

### 3. Top 5 Products by Rating

Identified products with the highest average customer ratings.

Top-rated products included:

| Product | Average Rating |
|---------|---------------:|
| Gloves | 3.86 |
| Sandals | 3.84 |
| Boots | 3.82 |
| Hat | 3.80 |
| Skirt | 3.78 |

### 4. Shipping Type Analysis

Compared average purchase amounts between Standard and Express shipping.

| Shipping Type | Average Purchase |
|---------------|-----------------:|
| Standard | $58.46 |
| Express | $60.48 |

Express-shipping customers showed a slightly higher average purchase amount.

### 5. Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers

Compared customer counts, average spending, and total revenue based on
subscription status.

| Subscription | Customers | Avg. Spend | Total Revenue |
|-------------|----------:|-----------:|--------------:|
| Yes | 1,053 | $59.49 | $62,645 |
| No | 2,847 | $59.87 | $170,436 |

### 6. Discount-Dependent Products

Identified products with the highest percentage of purchases involving discounts.

Top products included:

- Hat — 50.00%
- Sneakers — 49.66%
- Coat — 49.07%
- Sweater — 48.17%
- Pants — 47.37%

### 7. Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented according to their previous purchasing behavior.

| Segment | Customers |
|---------|----------:|
| Loyal | 3,116 |
| Returning | 701 |
| New | 83 |

### 8. Top Products by Category

Used SQL ranking techniques to identify the top three most purchased products
within each product category.

Examples:

- Accessories — Jewelry
- Clothing — Blouse / Pants
- Footwear — Sandals
- Outerwear — Jacket

### 9. Repeat Buyers & Subscription Behavior

Analyzed customers with more than five previous purchases to determine their
relationship with subscription status.

### 10. Revenue by Age Group

Analyzed revenue contribution across customer age segments.

| Age Group | Revenue |
|-----------|--------:|
| Young Adult | $62,143 |
| Middle-aged | $59,197 |
| Adult | $55,978 |
| Senior | $55,763 |

Young Adults generated the highest revenue among the analyzed age groups.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 👥 Customer Loyalty

The majority of customers fall into the Loyal segment, indicating a substantial
base of repeat shoppers.

### 💳 Subscription Opportunity

Only 1,053 of 3,900 customers are subscribers. This presents an opportunity to
convert existing loyal and repeat customers into subscribers.

### 🛒 Product Performance

Products such as Gloves, Sandals, and Boots received some of the highest average
customer ratings.

### 🏷️ Discount Behavior

Several products have close to half of their purchases associated with discounts,
suggesting that some product demand may be promotion-sensitive.

### 🚚 Shipping Behavior

Express-shipping customers had a slightly higher average purchase amount than
Standard-shipping customers.

### 👤 Age-Based Revenue

Young Adults generated the highest revenue among the analyzed age groups,
followed by Middle-aged customers.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

### 1. Increase Subscription Adoption

Promote exclusive subscriber benefits and target loyal repeat customers who have
not yet subscribed.

### 2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty Programs

Reward repeat buyers and encourage Returning customers to move into the Loyal
customer segment.

### 3. Optimize Discount Strategy

Review products with high discount dependency and balance promotional activity
with profitability.

### 4. Promote High-Performing Products

Highlight highly rated and frequently purchased products in marketing campaigns.

### 5. Use Targeted Marketing

Develop targeted campaigns based on customer age groups, purchasing behavior,
product preferences, and shipping preferences.
